Ordinary dialogue elicitation

The dialogue machine prompt

Target an intended completion, then ask a GPT to create fresh, reciprocal, ordinary conversations that genuinely arrive there. This page prepares the prompt; it does not claim to run an AI model itself.

The three questions guide construction privately. No philosophical vocabulary should appear in the characters' speech unless it belongs naturally to the situation.

The Pythagorean gateway

What remains through translation?

The square on the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares on the other two sides of a right triangle. Its particular geometrical relationship can be recognised across changing languages, proofs and generations. The Fiche Ceathar investigation asks what is common to the intelligible achievements through which it is understood.
